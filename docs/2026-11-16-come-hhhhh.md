As a small child in a town tucked into the Anti-Atlas mountains, crying on my mother’s back as she tried to lull me to sleep, nothing etched itself into my memory more deeply than the tender old lullaby *Bab n Yids*.

## Our Version of the Lullaby, Bab n Yids

#### *Nmaggarkh Bab n Yids Innayi Matrbit*

**ⵏⵎⴰⴳⴳⴰⵔⵅ  ⴱⴰⴱ  ⵏ  ⵢⵉⴷⵙ  ⵉⵏⵏⴰⵢⵉ  ⵎⴰⵜⵔⴱⵉⵜ**

I met the god of sleep, and he asked me, Who are you carrying ( on your back )?”



#### *Nnighas Ya’ Wwazzan, Innayi Snuhnnut.*

**ⵏⵏⵉⵖⴰⵙ  ⵢⴰ’ⵡⵡⴰⵣⵣⴰⵏ,  ⵉⵏⵏⴰⵢⵉ  ⵙⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓⵜ**

I told him a little kid, and he told me, “lull him.”



#### *Snuhnnut Snuhnnut, Snuhnnut Snuhnnut.*

**ⵙⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓⵜ  ⵙⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓⵜ,  ⵙⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓⵜ  ⵙⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓⵜ.**

lull him lull him, lull him lull him



#### *Nuhnnu, Nuhnnu, Nuhnnu, Nuhnnu*

**ⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓ ⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓ, ⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓ ⵏⵓⵀⵏⵏⵓ.**

Sleep Sleep, Sleep Sleep.

## An Alternative Version, Dadda Yids

#### *Nmaggarkh Dadda Yids Innayi Matrbit*

I met my lord Sleep, and he asked me, Who are you carrying ( on your back )?”

#### *Innayi Matribit, Nnighas Ayyur*

He asked me, “Who are you babysitting?” I told him Ayyur ( Moon )



#### *Immudda Wayyur, Nnighas Manza Lhdert*

Ayyur is gone. I asked him where the party was 

#### *Innayi han lhdert, han lhdert, dar mdn an*

He told me, “There it is, there it is, with those people.”



#### *A Rbikhk ay’Ayyur ar Fttukh ar Allakh*

Oh, Moon, I’m carrying you, walking and crying 

#### *Yaghk Lazz ay’Ayyur Imun dis Wala Ids*

You’re hungry, and you’re sleepy



#### *Yaghk Lazza Wala Ids Yili Osmmid gh ichnran*

You’re hungry, and you’re sleepy with cold lips

#### *Nmaggarkh Dadda Yids Innayi Matrbit*

I met my lord Sleep, and he asked me, Who are you carrying



#### *Nnighas Mad Rbikh Dadda Yids Ya’Wayyur*

I told him who I’m carrying, my lord, a Moon!

#### *Innayi Snuhnnut, Snuhnnut, Snuhnnut*

He told me, lull him, lull him, lull him

#### *nuhnnu nuhnnu nuhnnu nuhnnu*

Sleep, Sleep, Sleep, Sleep
